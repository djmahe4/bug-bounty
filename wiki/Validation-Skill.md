# Validation Skill (Wiki Summary)

Seven-question triage gate used before investing time in a full report. The operational skill lives in `skills/validation-skill.md`. Related operational pages: [[Recon-Methodology]] and [[AI-Agentic-Workflows]].

## The seven questions

1. Reproducible? — same result from a clean session at least twice.
2. In-scope? — asset and bug class explicitly allowed.
3. Exploitable? — not just a scanner hit or theoretical note.
4. Impact? — concrete “so what” for the program.
5. Duplicate? — searched recent reports and public write-ups.
6. Safe PoC? — minimal proof, no unnecessary data access or disruption.
7. Report-ready? — steps, evidence, and impact clear to a triager.

Answer “yes” to all seven before drafting a report. See the skill file for the full workflow and quality gate.
